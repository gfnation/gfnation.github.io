# Privacy Policy — Daily Sudoku

**Effective Date:** November 11, 2026

Daily Sudoku ("the App") is an ad-free, offline-first Sudoku game.

## Data Collection and Sharing
- **No Personal Data Collected:** The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage analytics, device identifiers, location data, or crash telemetry.
- **No Ads or Third-Party Tracking:** The App contains no advertisements and integrates no advertising or analytics SDKs.
- **No Network Permissions:** The App does not request internet permission. All daily puzzles are generated locally on your device.

## Local Device Storage
- Your daily puzzle boards, pencil marks, elapsed timers, streak counts, and optional feedback notes are stored locally on your device using Android private app storage (`SharedPreferences`).
- Uninstalling the App or clearing app data deletes this local storage.

## External Actions Initiated by You
- **Share:** If you tap Share, Android's system share sheet lets you send text via an app of your choice.
- **In-App Purchases:** If you choose to purchase the optional "Unlimited Hints Forever" one-time unlock ($4), the purchase is processed directly and securely by Google Play Billing under Google's Privacy Policy. The App does not collect, receive, or store your credit card or financial details.
- **Rate / Feedback:** If you choose to rate the App on Google Play or send optional email feedback, those actions are handled directly by Google Play or your email client under their respective privacy policies.

## Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the contact email listed on the Google Play store page.
