# Privacy Policy for chessduoku

**Last Updated:** November 25, 2026

Welcome to **chessduoku** ("we," "our," or "us"), developed by **eloguessr.net**. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the **chessduoku** mobile application (`com.chessdoku.app`).

---

## 1. Overview & Offline Play

You can play **chessduoku** completely offline without creating an account or providing personal information. Puzzle progress, streaks, timers, and unlocked pack status are stored locally on your device.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Optional Account Information
If you choose to sign in or create a shared **eloguessr.net** account inside the app, we collect:
- **Username**
- **Email address**
- **Password** (transmitted over HTTPS and stored securely on our servers)

### B. Gameplay & Progress Data
When you play **chessduoku**, we record:
- Puzzle numbers solved
- Elapsed solve time and check attempt counts
- Current and best solve streaks
- Unlocked puzzle pack entitlements

If you are signed in to an **eloguessr.net** account, your solved puzzle progress (puzzle number, completion status, elapsed time, and attempts) is synced with our servers (`https://elo-guessr.pages.dev`) so you can keep your progress across devices. If you are offline, progress is queued locally and synced when a connection is available.

### C. In-App Purchases
**chessduoku** offers optional one-time in-app purchases to unlock additional puzzle packs (Pack 1, puzzles #1–#100, is free). All payments are processed directly by **Google Play Billing**. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card number, billing address, or financial details. Google Play provides purchase tokens/entitlement status to verify and restore your unlocked packs.

### D. Permissions & Advertising
- **Internet Permission (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Used only for optional **eloguessr.net** account sign-in/sign-up, password reset, progress synchronization, and Google Play Billing.
- **No Third-Party Ads:** **chessduoku** does not display third-party advertisements or integrate advertising SDKs.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain **chessduoku**.
- Authenticate your optional **eloguessr.net** account and handle password reset requests.
- Sync and restore your puzzle progress across sessions and devices.
- Verify and restore your Google Play in-app purchases.

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

**chessduoku** relies on the following third-party service:
- **Google Play Services & Google Play Billing:** Used to process and restore in-app purchases in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 5. Data Security

All network communication between the **chessduoku** app and **eloguessr.net** servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Local game state is stored in private app storage on your device. While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

---

## 6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account and synced puzzle progress for as long as your account remains active. You can permanently delete your **eloguessr.net** account and all associated server data at any time:
- In the app: Open **Sign In / Account** → **Delete Account**, or
- Online: Visit [https://eloguessr.net/delete-account](https://eloguessr.net/delete-account).

To clear local data stored on your device, you can clear the app's storage in your Android device settings or uninstall the app.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

**chessduoku** is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an **eloguessr.net** account, please contact us so we can delete it.
